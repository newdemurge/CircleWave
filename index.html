Privacy Policy for Circle Wave

Last Updated: May 3, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Circle Wave ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("App") available on Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and the Australian Privacy Act.

Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • AdMob Data (Personal Information): When you use the App, Google AdMob, our third-party advertising partner, collects:
    • Identifiers: Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), IP address.
    • Internet Activity: Information about your interactions with ads.
    • Geolocation Data: Approximate location based on your IP address.
    • Inferences: Data used for ad targeting (e.g., inferred interests).
    This data is collected for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Local Game Data (Non-Personal Information): The App stores local game data (e.g., scores, selected themes, settings) on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs to personalize your experience. We do not consider this data to be personal information as it is not linked to your identity and is not shared with third parties.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect as follows:

  • AdMob Data: Used by Google AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, analyze ad performance, and prevent fraud, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy (linked below). This processing is based on your consent, which you provide when you start using the App and can withdraw at any time (see "Your Privacy Rights" below).
  • Local Game Data: Used solely on your device to save your game progress and preferences. This data is not transmitted off your device or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services and International Data Transfers

We use Google AdMob to serve ads in the App. AdMob collects and processes personal information as described above. As part of this process, your personal information may be transferred to the United States or other countries outside of your jurisdiction, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or Australia. Google ensures that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws, including the use of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as a legal safeguard. For more details on how Google handles your data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Data Retention Period

  • Local Game Data (PlayerPrefs): Stored on your device until you uninstall the App. This data can be removed by uninstalling the App.
  • AdMob Data: Stored by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. Google retains this data until you reset your Advertising ID or request deletion (see "Your Privacy Rights" below).

Compliance with Regulations

We comply with the following regulations:

  • GDPR (Europe), UK GDPR (United Kingdom): We act as a Data Controller for AdMob data, and processing is based on your consent.
  • U.S. State Privacy Laws (e.g., CCPA): We collect identifiers, internet activity, geolocation data, and inferences via AdMob, which may be considered a "sale" of personal information under CCPA.
  • PIPEDA (Canada), Australian Privacy Act.

Your Privacy Rights

You have certain rights regarding your personal information, which can be exercised through the options available in the App’s “Settings” menu or by following the instructions below:

  • Language Selection: Change the App language (English, Polish, German, French, Spanish) using the “Language” button in the Settings menu.
  • Privacy Policy: You are currently viewing this policy.
  • Local Game Data (PlayerPrefs): Local game data (e.g., scores, themes) are stored on your device and are not considered personal information. You can remove this data by uninstalling the App.
  • Access and Deletion of AdMob Data (All Users): For personal information collected by Google AdMob (e.g., device identifiers, IP addresses):
    • Access: You can request access to your data directly from Google to understand what information is collected about you.
    • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data directly from Google at https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form.
    • Alternatively, you can reset your Advertising ID in your device settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads on Android) to stop AdMob from using your previous ID for personalized ads.
  • Opt-Out of Data Sale (CCPA): California residents can opt out of the sale of their personal information by selecting the “Do Not Sell My Personal Information” option in the Settings menu. Under CCPA, sharing data with AdMob for personalized ads may be considered a "sale."
  • Withdraw Consent (GDPR/UK GDPR): Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK can withdraw consent for AdMob to use their data for personalized ads by selecting the “Withdraw Consent” option in the Settings menu.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint (GDPR/UK GDPR): If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office – UODO; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office – ICO).

Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us at the email below, and we will promptly delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page, and the “Last Updated” date at the top will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
