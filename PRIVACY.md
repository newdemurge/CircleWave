## Personal Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information:
- **AdMob Data (Personal Information)**: When you use the App, Google AdMob, our third-party advertising partner, collects:
  - **Identifiers**: Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID), IP address.
  - **Internet Activity**: Information about your interactions with ads.
  - **Geolocation Data**: Approximate location based on your IP address.
  - **Inferences**: Data used for ad targeting (e.g., inferred interests).
  This data is collected for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.
- **Local Game Data (Non-Personal Information)**: The App stores local game data (e.g., scores, selected themes, settings) on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs to personalize your experience. We do not consider this data to be personal information as it is not linked to your identity and is not shared with third parties.

## How We Use Your Information
- **AdMob Data**: Used by Google AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, analyze ad performance, and prevent fraud, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy (linked below).
- **Local Game Data**: Used solely on your device to save your game progress and preferences. This data is not transmitted off your device or shared with third parties.

## Third-Party Services
We use Google AdMob to serve ads in the App. AdMob collects and processes personal information as described above. For more details on how Google handles your data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## Data Retention Period
- **Local Game Data (PlayerPrefs)**: Stored on your device until you uninstall the App. This data can be removed by uninstalling the App.
- **AdMob Data**: Stored by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. Google retains this data until you reset your Advertising ID or request deletion (see "Your Privacy Rights" below).

## Your Privacy Rights
You have certain rights regarding your personal information, which can be exercised through the options available in the App’s “Settings” menu or by following the instructions below:
- **Language Selection**: Change the App language (English, Polish, German, French, Spanish) using the “Language” button in the Settings menu.
- **Privacy Policy**: View this policy by clicking “Privacy Policy” in the Settings menu, which directs you to our GitHub page ([https://newdemurge.github.io/CircleWave/PRIVACY.md](https://newdemurge.github.io/CircleWave/PRIVACY.md)).
- **Local Game Data (PlayerPrefs)**: Local game data (e.g., scores, themes) are stored on your device and are not considered personal information. You can remove this data by uninstalling the App.
- **Access and Deletion of AdMob Data**: For personal information collected by Google AdMob (e.g., device identifiers, IP addresses):
  - **Access**: You can request access to your data directly from Google.
  - **Deletion**: You can request deletion of your data directly from Google at [https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form](https://support.google.com/policies/contact/general_privacy_form).
  - Alternatively, you can reset your Advertising ID in your device settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy > Advertising on iOS) to stop AdMob from using your previous ID for personalized ads.
- **Opt-Out (CCPA)**: California residents can opt out of the sale of their personal information by selecting the “Do Not Sell My Personal Information” option in the Settings menu.
- **Withdraw Consent (GDPR/UK GDPR)**: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK can withdraw consent for AdMob to use their data for personalized ads by selecting the “Withdraw Consent” option in the Settings menu.
